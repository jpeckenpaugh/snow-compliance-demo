Clauses carry Yes/No answersExtractive spans: answers[{text, answer_start}] + is_impossibleDerive presence from is_impossible
Clause text with start/end offsetsOnly answer_start; end must be derived from the text lengthDerive end; store snippet
Categories like Indemnification, Limitation of Liability, Confidentiality, WarrantiesNone of these exist; liability is Uncapped Liability / Cap On LiabilityRemap the rubric

The headline finding Four clause categories named in the original brainstorm don't exist in CUAD. The risk rubric was remapped to real categories (Uncapped Liability, Cap On Liability, Termination For Convenience, Anti-Assignment, Change Of Control, Minimum Commitment, Governing Law, Non-Compete, Exclusivity).
