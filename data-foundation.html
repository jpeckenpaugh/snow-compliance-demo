Source A

The CUAD dataset

The Contract Understanding Atticus Dataset is a legal AI benchmark: 510 commercial contracts annotated across 41 clause categories (the CUAD v1 distribution ships the contracts and annotations as JSON). Each annotation carries the clause text span plus an expert Yes/No answer for whether the clause type is present.

What we extract per contract

  • contract_name, contract_type (NDA, license, service, etc.), party names.
  • Per clause category: answer (Yes/No/NA), annotated text span, character offsets.

Source B

The synthetic law-firm layer (Faker)

Faker (Python) generates deterministic, obviously-fictional records. We fix a random seed so every rebuild is reproducible and board reviewers see stable data.

Data model

Logical relationships

Contracts and the business layer meet at a bridge, because a contract can serve more than one matter and a matter can hold more than one contract.

client 1---* matter *---* contract (via contract_attachment)
  matter 1---* fact_clause *---1 dim_contract
  matter 1---* matter_authorization *---1 employee
  matter 1---* matter_assignment   *---1 employee
  contract 1---* fact_clause *---1 dim_clause_category

Trust

Data-quality checks

Every load ends with referential-integrity and completeness assertions. Failures are surfaced, never silently dropped.

-- Orphaned clauses (no matching contract)
SELECT f.contract_id, COUNT(*)
FROM core.fact_clause f
LEFT JOIN core.dim_contract c USING (contract_id)
WHERE c.contract_id IS NULL
GROUP BY 1;

-- Matters with no authorized attorney (governance gap)
SELECT m.matter_id
FROM core.dim_matter m
LEFT JOIN core.matter_authorization a USING (matter_id)
WHERE a.matter_id IS NULL;

-- Clause coverage vs CUAD's 41 categories
SELECT COUNT(DISTINCT clause_category_id) AS categories_present
FROM core.fact_clause;

-- Contracts attached to more than one matter (expected for the bridge)
SELECT contract_id, COUNT(DISTINCT matter_id) AS matters
FROM core.bridge_matter_contract GROUP BY 1 HAVING matters > 1;
